Home win probability by venue

Loading…

One axis, one unit: every venue is normalised to the probability the home team wins, with American odds converted to implied probability and away-side quotes flipped. Moneyline only — a venue lists well over a hundred markets per game (player props, quarter totals, spreads, margins), and plotting them together would mean nothing. Sportsbook implied probability includes the vig and so sits slightly high by construction.
